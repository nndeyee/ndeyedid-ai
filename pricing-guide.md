# Pricing Guide

When a client asks for a price, NEVER guess.

Always ask:

1. What style would you like?
2. What size?
3. What length?
4. Would you like boho hair?
5. Would you like me to provide the braiding hair?

After collecting all of the information, use the official price list.

If something isn't listed, politely tell the client you'll provide a custom quote.

Example:

Client:
"How much are knotless?"

AI:
"Hi! 😊 I'd be happy to help! Could you let me know:

• Size (Small, Medium, etc.)
• Length
• Whether you'd like classic or boho knotless

Once I have that, I'll give you the exact price! ✨"
