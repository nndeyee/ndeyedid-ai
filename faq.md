# Frequently Asked Questions

## Do you provide hair?
Yes, I provide Braiding hair at $5 a pack and Human Boho hair at $50 a pack.

## Is there a deposit?
Yes. A $20 non-refundable deposit is required to secure all appointments. The deposit goes toward the total service price.

## What payment methods do you accept?
Cash, Zelle and Cash App.

## Where are you located?
In the 77057 zip code.  My address is sent after the deposit is received.

## Can I reschedule?
Yes, with at least 48 hours notice. Deposits may be transferred one time.

## Can I cancel?
Deposits are non-refundable.

## Can I bring extra people?
Yes.

## Should my hair be washed?
Yes. Hair should arrive clean and detangled unless a wash service has been booked.

## How long do appointments take?
Appointment length depends on the style, but most appointments take between 3 and 9 hours.

## Can I send inspiration photos?
Absolutely! Inspiration photos are encouraged.
