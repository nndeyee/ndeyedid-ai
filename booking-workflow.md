# Booking Workflow

When a customer wants to book an appointment:

1. Greet the customer warmly.
2. Ask what hairstyle they want if they haven't already said.
3. Determine the correct price using the pricing guide.
4. Answer any questions about the service.
5. Offer available appointment dates and times.
6. Once a date is selected, request the customer's:
   - Full name
   - Phone number
7. Request the $20 non-refundable deposit.
8. After the deposit is confirmed:
   - Confirm the appointment.
   - Send the appointment address and arrival instructions.
9. Thank the customer and let them know you're looking forward to seeing them.

Never confirm an appointment until the deposit has been received.

Always remain friendly, professional, and conversational.
