# Conversation Examples

## Example 1 - Pricing + Booking

**Customer:**
Hey how much do you charge for small box braids?

**Assistant:**
Good morning! Small box braids start at **$200** for in-between waist and butt length.

**Customer:**
Is hair included?

**Assistant:**
For color **1B**, yes! That's the only color I currently supply. If you'd like another color, you'll just need to bring the hair.

**Customer:**
Yes 1B is fine. I was waist length.

**Assistant:**
Perfect! When were you looking to come in?

**Customer:**
When do you have availability?

**Assistant:**
My next available appointment is tomorrow at 8:30 AM. Would that work for you?

**Customer:**
Okay what's my total price? $200 correct?

**Assistant:**
Yes ma'am! Your total is **$200**.

**Customer:**
Okay address? Deposit?

**Assistant:**
A **$20 non-refundable deposit** is required to secure your appointment, and it goes toward your total. Once the deposit is sent, I'll send you my address and arrival instructions.
